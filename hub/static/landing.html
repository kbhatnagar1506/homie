The problem

Renting from 8,000 miles away
is a wall.

Every year, over a million international students try to sign a US lease before they land. The system was not built for them.

01

They can't call you back.

Leasing offices won't dial a +91 or +86 number. You miss the callback, you miss the unit.

Calls phones them from a US line.
02

Deals hide on certain days.

Look-and-lease specials, fee waivers, end-of-month drops. You hear about them after you sign.

Negotiator asks, every time.
03

No SSN, no lease.

Buildings demand a US guarantor you don't have, or a year of rent up front.

Papers finds the no-SSN path.
04

Cashier's check only.

Deposit due in person, by paper check, before you even have a US bank account.

Homie pays only when keys are handed over.
05

Repairs go to a bot.

Portal chatbots close tickets without anyone ever looking at the leak under your sink.

Fix looks at it with you, on a live video call.

01 · Homie

Searches real buildings, live.

Google Places finds every building near campus. Homie reads each website for today's prices, not last month's listing.

places.search("apartments near North Campus") → 14 buildings
site.read 14/14 → 6 under $1,600
02 · Pics

Screenshots every listing.

Floor plans, unit photos and the fine print, captured so you see exactly what the office sees.

03 · Calls

Phones the leasing office.

A real call over Twilio with Gemini Live voice. It always says up front that it is an AI assistant calling on your behalf.

> "Hi, I'm an AI assistant calling for a student moving from India. Is the 1BR on floor 4 still open?"
04 · Later

Office closed? It calls back.

Later schedules the follow-up for when they open, in their timezone, while you sleep in yours.

schedule callback Tue 9:05am ET (6:35pm IST)
05 · Negotiator

Asks for the deal you'd never know about.

Application fee waivers, the Tuesday special, a free month on a 13-month lease. It asks, politely, every time.

✓ app fee waived · ✓ $200 off first month
06 · Papers + Policy

Handles the no-SSN paperwork.

Passport, I-20 and offer letter instead of an SSN. Guarantor services if needed. Policy checks the lease against local tenant law.

07 · Memory

Remembers you.

Your budget, your move date, that you hate ground floors. Next time, nobody asks twice.

08 · Fix

Sticks around after move-in.

Something breaks? Show Fix on a live video call. It files the repair with photos and follows up until it's done.
