# Homie Caller
Phones US leasing offices on behalf of international students through ElevenLabs voice agents and Twilio. Always says it is an AI assistant. Collects price, discount days, fees, payment rules and what the building accepts instead of an SSN, and places negotiation and repair calls. Part of the Homie team (Homie, Caller, Negotiator, Paperwork, Repairs, Policy).
