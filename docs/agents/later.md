# Homie Later

The waiting agent. It holds future tasks ("get offers Monday", "call The Mix when they open", "remind me to pay rent on the 1st", "check the repair tomorrow"). Jev classifies each task, Gemini works out the exact time in Atlanta, and Later wakes up on time and hands the task to the right agent. It takes over automatically when a round of calls ends with offices closed. Tasks survive restarts.

Part of Homie, a 10-agent Fetch.ai team that's an international student's person in America: Homie, Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later and Scout.
