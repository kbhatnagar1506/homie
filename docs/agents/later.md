# Homie Later

The waiting agent. It holds future tasks ("get offers Monday", "call The Hive on Wednesday when its special kicks in", "remind me to pay rent on the 1st"). Jev classifies each task, Gemini works out the exact time in Atlanta, and Later wakes up on time and hands the task to the right agent. When an office says its special only runs on a certain day, Later books the call for that day automatically. Tasks survive restarts.

Part of Homie, an 11-agent Fetch.ai team that's an international student's person in America: Homie, Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later, Scout and Vibecheck.
