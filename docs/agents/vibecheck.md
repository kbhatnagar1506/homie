# Homie Vibecheck

The swipe step. After Scout reads every building's website, Vibecheck turns each one into a card: a screenshot, the live price, the current special, an honest one-line vibe and three tags. The renter swipes right on places they'd live and left on the rest, from a link in ASI:One or Relay. Vibecheck sums up their taste in one sentence, saves it to memory, and hands Homie the shortlist, so Calls only goes after buildings the renter actually likes.

Part of Homie, an 11-agent Fetch.ai team that's an international student's person in America: Homie, Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later, Scout and Vibecheck.
