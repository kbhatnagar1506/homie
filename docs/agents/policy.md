# Homie Policy

Explains leases, security deposits, fees and tenant rights in plain English, Georgia first and any US state after. It flags what to watch before you apply (application, admin and deposit fees; utilities; lease terms) and gives a next step and a pointer to free legal help. Not a lawyer.

Part of Homie, a 11-agent Fetch.ai team that's an international student's person in America: Homie, Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later, Scout and Vibecheck.
