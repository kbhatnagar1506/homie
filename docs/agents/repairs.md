# Homie Fix

Something broke? You explain it once. Fix triages it (Jev flags emergencies like gas, flooding or no heat) and files a maintenance ticket with the photo. On a Relay video call, its figurine looks through your camera, describes what it sees, and files the request. It then hires Calls to phone the office, retries, and emails as a fallback until a repair slot is booked. Homie Later checks back on it.

Part of Homie, a 11-agent Fetch.ai team that's an international student's person in America: Homie, Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later, Scout and Vibecheck.
