# Homie: your person in America

An AI team for international students renting in the US from abroad. Homie finds, calls and negotiates your apartment, handles the no-SSN paperwork, chases repairs, remembers you, and picks up the work again when the office opens.

**Try:**
- "Get me a one bedroom at The Mix, jointhemix.com, Aug 20. I have no SSN."
- "I move to downtown Atlanta Aug 20. 1-bed under $2,500, no SSN. Book it if there's a month free."
- "My ice maker is broken."
- "Can my landlord keep my whole deposit for one scratch?"
- "Call them back Monday when they open."

**What happens:**
1. Homie reads your message (Jev decides route, bedrooms, no-SSN and urgency in one call) and recalls what it knows about you (Mapi).
2. Homie searches real buildings (Google Places) and Scout reads every one of their websites: live prices, specials, office hours, application link, international rules.
3. Vibe check: you get a link to swipe right or left on each building (photos, live price, special). Vibecheck learns your taste and only the ones you like go forward.
4. Calls phones every office you liked at once (ElevenLabs voice, always disclosed as an AI) and asks when each special applies; Later books a callback for that day. Negotiator plays the best offer against the rest.
4. Papers works out exactly what to send instead of an SSN. Policy flags fees and tenant law.
5. If offices are closed, Pics reads prices off their websites and Later calls back when they open.
6. Homie holds the best deal and Papers starts your real application live in a browser, stopping before the password and submit, which stay yours. After move-in, Fix handles repairs, including over a video call where it can see the problem.

You only pay when you get your keys (Fetch Payment Protocol).

**The team (11 uAgents):** Homie (orchestrator), Calls, Negotiator, Papers, Fix, Policy, Pics, Memory, Later, Scout, Vibecheck. All of them are also live in the Relay app as a group chat you can text or video-call.
